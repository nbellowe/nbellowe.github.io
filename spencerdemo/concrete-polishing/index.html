Questions

Coating or polishing: which should I get?

Polishing grinds and densifies the concrete itself, so there's no coating to peel. It works well in basements, shops, and commercial spaces. Coatings add color, texture, and chemical resistance, and are usually the better call for garages. Spencer will tell you which he'd pick for your slab.

Can any concrete be polished?

Most can, but the result depends on the slab. Old patches, heavy stains, and soft concrete all show through. Spencer will look at it first and tell you what it'll look like.

Do you seal patios and porches?

Yes: cleaning, repairs, and penetrating sealers that help exterior concrete handle Colorado freeze-thaw cycles and deicers.
