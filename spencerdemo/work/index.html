Project · Denver garage

A purple garage, tuned with the owners

The owners wanted something bold: a purple base under gray, charcoal, and white flake.

  • They chose the look as it developed. Photos and video at each step let them decide how much purple should show through the flake, before the topcoat locked it in.
  • A change partway through, to carbon on the strip visible outside the garage door, was agreed in writing and done.
  • Extra time where it counted. Spencer added days to sand and refine the surface before sealing, so the finish met his standard.
