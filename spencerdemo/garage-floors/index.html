Systems

Flake

A colored base with vinyl flake broadcast over it, then a clear topcoat. It hides dirt, adds grip, and comes in endless color blends. It's the most popular garage floor.

Quartz broadcast

Colored quartz sand in place of flake, for a harder-wearing, more textured surface. Good for shops and heavier use.

Polyaspartic topcoats

A clear topcoat that resists UV yellowing and cures faster than epoxy. A common choice over flake in garages that get sun.

Metallic & custom

Metallic pigments and custom color work for a one-off floor, in basements, showrooms, or a garage you want to hang out in.

Where they go: garages, basements, home gyms, workshops, and utility rooms. For businesses, see commercial floors.

The process

What actually happens to your floor

Here's what we do, in order.

  1. Walkthrough & estimateLook at the slab, check for moisture and old sealers, and ask how you use the space. Then you get a written estimate.
  2. Mechanical prepGrind the surface to open up the concrete so the coating bonds to the slab, not to dust or old sealer.
  3. RepairsFill cracks, pits, and spalls, and deal with the joints, so the finish is flat and nothing shows through.
  4. Base coat & broadcastBase color down, then flake or quartz broadcast. You get photos and video before anything is locked in.
  5. Scrape, sand, inspectKnock down loose flake and high spots and look it over. Anything that isn't right gets fixed now, before the topcoat.
  6. Topcoat & walkthroughA clear topcoat, then a final walkthrough together with care instructions.

Common questions

What does a garage floor coating cost?

It depends on the square footage, the condition of the concrete, and the system you choose. Send a few photos and rough dimensions and you'll get an estimate, usually with a couple of options.

Will I get a written quote?

Yes. You get a written estimate before any work starts, covering the scope, prep, your colors or blend, the timeline, the price, and the payment terms. Changes along the way are written down with their price impact before the work is done.

Can I see my colors before committing?

Yes. Mix a blend in the online blend builder or pick a stock blend, and Spencer will make a physical sample to look at on your own floor.

Epoxy or polyaspartic: which is better?

Both have a place. Epoxy builds thickness and bonds well. Polyaspartic topcoats resist UV yellowing and cure faster. Many garage systems use both. Spencer will tell you which he'd use on your floor.

How long until I can park on it?

That depends on the system and the temperature. You get specific walk-on and park-on times for your floor before work starts.

My concrete has cracks and oil stains. Is that a problem?

Usually not, as long as they're dealt with first. Cracks get repaired and stains cleaned or ground out during prep. If something can't be fixed properly, you'll hear that up front.

Can you do it in winter?

Coatings need the slab and the air warm enough to cure. Cold months suit basements, heated shops, and interior floors, and garages get booked for warmer stretches. Ask early for spring dates.
