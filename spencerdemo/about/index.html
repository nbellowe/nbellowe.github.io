About

Hi, I'm Spencer.

I own Denver Surface Solutions, and I'm the one on your job.

Before floors, I spent 15 years in oil & gas and petrochemical processing. In that work you don't skip steps. You figure out exactly what you're working with, use the right materials for it, and do it the same careful way every time.

I run surfaces the same way. I look closely at what you have before I quote it, I put the plan in writing, and I send photos and video as the work goes, so you can weigh in while things can still change.

If something isn't right, I fix it before I call it done. Sometimes that takes an extra day, and I'd rather spend it than hand over a floor I'm not proud of.

It's your home. Every detail matters.
