Why coatings fail

Most peeling garage floors weren't prepped right.

The coating usually isn't the problem. The concrete wasn't ground, cleaned, or repaired well enough first, often to finish in one day.

Spencer spent 15 years in oil & gas and petrochemical processing before starting Denver Surface Solutions. In that work, a skipped step means a leak or a shutdown. He preps floors with the same care.
