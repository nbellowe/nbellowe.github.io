Why coatings fail

The coating is only as good as the prep under it.

When a garage floor peels, the product usually isn't to blame. Prep got rushed to hit a one-day schedule.

Spencer spent 15 years in oil & gas and petrochemical processing before starting Denver Surface Solutions. That's work where skipping a step means something fails. He runs every floor the same way: prep it properly, check each coat, and don't move on until it's right.

Custom blends & samples

See your colors before anything is ordered.

Pick a stock blend or mix your own in the blend builder. Spencer makes a physical sample board in your blend, so you can set it on your own floor, in your own light, and decide.

On one recent garage, the owners wanted a purple base. Spencer sent photos and video at each step, and they chose how much purple showed through the flake. When they asked partway through for a different color on the strip outside the garage door, it got written down and done.
