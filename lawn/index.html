How it works

Four steps, then you stop thinking about it

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit, measure, and look at what the yard is actually doing — sun, slope, soil, irrigation coverage, problem corners.

  2. Your plan

    A written schedule and a flat monthly price covering visits, treatments and routine supplies. No contract to sign.

  3. Scheduled service

    We come on a set day. You get a note when we have been by, including anything we noticed or fixed.

  4. Seasonal adjustment

    Frequency and treatments shift with the season — heavier in June, a different job entirely in October. We adjust; you do not have to ask.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Do I need to own a mower or any equipment?

No. We bring every piece of equipment to each visit and take it with us when we leave. That is the whole idea — your garage stays empty and you never maintain a mower again.

Who pays for fertilizer, seed and mulch?

Routine supplies are included in your monthly plan. Larger materials — a sod replacement, a load of stone, a new irrigation zone — are quoted separately and only bought after you approve the number.

If you buy equipment or parts for my yard, who owns it?

Equipment we bring and take away stays ours. Anything permanently installed on your property — sprinkler heads, valves, drip line, edging, plantings — belongs to you once it is in the ground and paid for.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. Tell us you are done and we will finish out the month you have paid for.

What happens when it rains, hails or snows?

We do not mow a saturated lawn — it tears the turf and ruts the soil. Rained-out visits move to the next dry day at no extra charge. After a hailstorm or a late spring snow we prioritize cleanup and damage assessment for customers on a plan.

Do you work around watering restrictions?

Yes. We schedule treatments and irrigation work around current restrictions, and we will tell you when a stretch of turf would be cheaper and healthier converted to xeriscape than kept on a schedule.

Is it safe for pets and kids?

Tell us before we treat and we will note it on your file. We let you know which product went down and how long to stay off the lawn, and we latch every gate behind us.

How does billing work?

One flat charge each month for your plan. Approved extras are billed separately and itemized, so you always know what a charge was for.
